How I Check an Alert

Click any step below to see what I look at before closing or escalating an alert.

STEP 1

Check What Triggered

Look at who, which computer, and what time the alert fired. An alert is only a clue, not proof of a hack on its own.

Example: Confirm username, device IP (10.16.56.241), and which program started the action.
